About this demo

A prototype of a one-stop department hub. Meeting recordings become briefs with tracked action items, and project requests become approved projects with a baseline, a target and a measured result.

Everything is clickable: change statuses and owners, submit and approve a project request, or look one up as a requester. All people and companies are fictional, and your changes stay in your own browser.

Real build:Wispr / Fathom→AI summary→Forms or Jotform→Power Automate / Make→SharePoint→Power BI

My team

Tick the people on your team. Deliverables owned by them get an "Our team" tag, and you can assign any deliverable to a teammate from its owner dropdown.
